# Marnie's Grimmsnarl ex v2 strategy

## Primary plan

1. Go first.
2. Establish two Impidimp routes, one Munkidori, and one Snorunt route when the hand permits.
3. Complete the first Grimmsnarl ex quickly.
4. Use Punk Up to make the current attacker live and seed one real backup route.
5. Manually attach Darkness Energy to Munkidori as soon as a concrete Grimmsnarl route is visible.
6. Attack with Shadow Bullet every turn once the current attacker and one backup are secured.
7. Coordinate Bench 30 and Adrena-Brain into exact prize thresholds.

## Route rules

- Impidimp + Rare Candy + Grimmsnarl is a complete route; do not search Morgrem just because it is absent.
- Punk Up targets the body that can actually evolve with the visible hand.
- A second Grimmsnarl is worth completing before attacking.
- A third Grimmsnarl line, an extra Morgrem, or Froslass development falls below a live Shadow Bullet.
- Chip attacks remain fallback actions and are not promoted over the main route.

## Crustle route

- Treat Shadow Bullet's Active damage as zero into Crustle.
- Use Adrena-Brain to place counters onto Crustle or Dwebble.
- Use Boss's Orders for a real KO on Mega Kangaskhan ex, Dwebble, or another valuable target.
- Shadow Bullet can remain useful for its Bench 30, but it is no longer scored as a normal 180-damage attack.

## Damage-map priorities

1. Immediate 30-counter or Bench-30 KO.
2. Put the Active into Shadow Bullet range.
3. Remove a pre-evolution body before it becomes an attacker or draw engine.
4. Remove Munkidori, Fezandipiti ex, Dudunsparce, or another engine.
5. In damage-prevention matchups, build the counter route instead of repeatedly attacking for zero.

## Resource priorities

- Night Stretcher: Darkness Energy first when Munkidori is unpowered or the backup route lacks Energy.
- Poké Pad: current route deficit, not a fixed card order.
- Spikemuth Gym: Grimmsnarl, Morgrem, or Impidimp according to the missing stage.
- Petrel: exact board establishment, evolution, recovery, or profitable Boss route.
