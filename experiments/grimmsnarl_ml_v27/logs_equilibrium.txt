logit P(win) = 5.674 -0.00586 * settled_opponent_score

  our implied rating (where the curve crosses 50%): 969

  a slope steeper than Elo means we fall off faster than a
  rating-consistent agent would: the stronger the opponent, the
  worse we do relative to what our own rating predicts.
